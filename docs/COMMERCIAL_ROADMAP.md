## 2. Institutional Integrations Roadmap

### 2.1 Learning Management Systems (LMS / LTI 1.3)
- Direct assignment launch from Canvas, Blackboard, or Moodle.
- Automatic gradebook passback of lab scores and completed challenge rubrics via LTI Advantage 1.3.

### 2.2 Multi-User Collaborative Laboratory
- Shared WebRTC data channel rooms allowing two remote students to collaborate on the same optical bench in WebXR.
- Synchronized laser beam manipulation and real-time audio chat.

### 2.3 Release Milestones:
- **v1.0 (Current MVP):** 3 complete validated experiments, curated glass database, 3D laboratory, Quest 3S WebXR, CSV/JSON exports, printable PDF reports.
- **v1.1 (Q1 2027):** Stations D (Polarization), E (Michelson Interferometer), and F (Fiber Optics) activated end-to-end.
- **v1.2 (Q2 2027):** Canvas/Moodle LTI 1.3 gradebook sync and instructor dashboard.
